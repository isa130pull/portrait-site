RollingGirlzShe rolls. She can’t stop.

RollingGirlz is a silly 3D dodge game. Tap left or right to steer a girl who tumbles forward forever past buildings, rocks, and, for some reason, giants standing in the sea. Your score is how many times she rolled. Dress her up and climb the online leaderboard.

FeaturesAll apps and web tools

How to play

That was pretty much the whole tutorial.

Tap left. Tap right. That’s it.

Tap the left side of the screen to roll left and the right side to roll right. Forward movement is automatic, so one hand is enough. Crashing into things costs HP, and HP also drains slowly over time.

The more she spins, the better

Your score is how many times she rolled (ROLL). Pick up items along the way to spin more and go faster.

  • Heart: restores HP
  • Rolling: more spin
  • Speed: faster forward movement

Feel the SPEED and SPIN

Meters show her speed (km/h) and spin (rpm). When she accelerates, the view widens and speed lines rush past, and her trail changes color as the spin climbs. Your top speed and top spin are recorded on the result screen.

A clear bonus for the brave

Make it to the goal and your roll count gets a ×1.10 to ×1.30 bonus based on the HP you have left. The bonus-applied number is what the leaderboard uses.

Dress her up, make it sillier

Kitty, witch, ninja, princess… dress up the rolling girl however you like. Outfits unlock as you play more and roll more. Costumes are cosmetic only, so speed and score are the same for everyone. Rumor has it something else can roll too…?

Global leaderboard

Compete with players around the world and set your own name (up to 8 characters) to climb the ranks. On iPhone, Game Center leaderboards and achievements are supported too.

The scenery gets weirder

She tumbles through a picture-book diorama world. Each stage is faster than the last, and the music changes with it.

Game screens

From the controls tutorial to the goal, results, and leaderboard. On a phone, swipe left or right to view the images.

Get RollingGirlz

Play on iPhone and Android. Free to play with ads; an in-app purchase removes the ads.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is it?

It is a silly 3D dodge game. You tap left or right to steer a girl who can’t stop rolling, dodge obstacles, and aim for the goal across three stages: a pastel town, a sunset wasteland, and a sea of giants.

How do I control the game?

Tap the left or right side of the screen to change direction. Forward movement is automatic.

How is the score decided?

Your score is how many times she rolled. Spin items add to the count, and reaching the goal applies a ×1.10 to ×1.30 clear bonus depending on your remaining HP.

What do the items do?

Hearts restore HP, rolling items increase spin, and speed items increase forward speed.

Do outfits cost money?

Outfits unlock through play, such as play count and total rolls. They are cosmetic only: speed and score are identical for every outfit, so the leaderboard stays fair.

How do I join the leaderboard?

Enter a name (up to 8 characters) from the leaderboard screen. The leaderboard updates when you are online. If you also enjoy tap-based destruction, try House Breaker!!.

Which languages are supported?

Japanese, English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, and French. The game follows your device language and you can change it in settings.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The core game is free with ads, and an in-app purchase removes the ads. It is available from the App Store and Google Play.
