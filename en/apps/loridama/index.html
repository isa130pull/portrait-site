RollingGirlzAction with left and right taps

Tap the left or right side of the screen to change the rolling girl’s direction in RollingGirlz. Forward movement is automatic, so you can focus on dodging obstacles and choosing items. Reach the goals in three stages and compete for a score in the nationwide rankings.

FeaturesAll apps and web tools

Controls and items

Tap to change direction

Tap the left or right side of the screen to turn. The girl moves forward automatically, leaving you to watch the obstacles ahead and adjust her route.

Pick items for HP and abilities

Hearts restore HP, rolling items increase rotation speed, and speed items increase forward movement. Choose among these three types as you plan your route and manage remaining HP.

Reach all three goals

The stages get faster and more difficult as you progress. Dodge obstacles and preserve your HP to reach all three goals. You can also compete in nationwide score rankings.

Game screens

See the stage obstacles, items, and score rankings. On a phone, swipe left or right to view the images.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a tap-controlled obstacle-dodging game?

Yes. Forward movement is automatic; tap the left or right side to change direction. Dodge obstacles, choose hearts and ability boosts, and aim for the finish across three stages.

Can I compete for scores after finishing the stages?

You can challenge the national rankings with your score. If you also want to try tap-based destruction, visit House Breaker’s free web challenge.

How do I control the game?

Tap the left or right side of the screen to steer the rolling girl. Forward movement is automatic.

What do the items do?

Hearts restore HP, rolling items increase rotation speed, and speed items increase forward speed.

How many stages are there?

There are three. The speed increases as you progress, so avoiding obstacles requires faster decisions.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The core game is free to play and is available from the App Store and Google Play.
