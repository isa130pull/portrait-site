SushiBombLaunch sushi to destroy buildings

SushiBomb is a 30-second action game where you tap to launch sushi at buildings. Collect 36 varieties, including tuna and salmon, upgrade them, and work through nine stages. You can also play time attacks and compete in nationwide rankings.

How to playAll apps and web tools

Collecting sushi and playing

Tap to launch sushi

Tap the screen to launch your selected sushi at a spot in the building. Aim at the building and its equipment to increase the damage total within 30 seconds.

Collect 36 varieties

There are 36 varieties to collect, including tuna, salmon, shrimp, and salmon roe. Once you unlock a variety, you can select it for your next run.

Upgrade and clear stages

Upgrade power, size, rapid fire, and sushi unlocks. Work through nine stages, play time attacks, or compete for a score in the nationwide rankings.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play in a web browser?

SushiBomb is an app for iPhone and Android. It is free to download and play. On this page, you can see sushi smash buildings in the screenshots and gameplay video.

Can I enjoy both sushi collecting and destruction?

Yes. The app combines throwing sushi to break buildings with collecting and upgrading 36 kinds of sushi. If you prefer destroying furniture and appliances, you can also try House Breaker.

Are the controls difficult?

No. Tap the screen to launch sushi, so you can start playing immediately with one hand.

How many kinds of sushi are there?

There are 36, including tuna, salmon, shrimp, and salmon roe.

What can I play besides the main stages?

Along with nine stages, the game includes a time-attack mode and nationwide score rankings.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The core game is free to play and is available from the App Store and Google Play.
