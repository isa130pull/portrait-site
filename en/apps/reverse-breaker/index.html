How to play Reverse Breaker

Tap to bomb the balls

Tap anywhere to place a bomb and let the blast pop the balls. Bombs are limited, but you get them back when you pop balls. When every block is gone, it's game over. Protect them through all 10 stages to clear the game.

Chain combos for FEVER

Pop balls back to back to build your combo and score big. At 10 combos, FEVER begins with bigger blasts and a rainbow screen. Hit a ball dead center for PERFECT, or pop several at once for BOOM!

Costumes and rankings

Dress your blocks in 12 costumes, unlocked with stars from daily missions and play milestones. Take on the weekly and all-time rankings. As the stages go on, the sky changes from day to sunset to night.

Game screens

See FEVER mode, the changing stages, costumes, the ranking, and the results screen. On a phone, swipe left or right to view the images.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is it?

It's Breakout in reverse. Instead of bouncing a ball to break blocks, you blast the incoming balls with bombs to protect the blocks below.

Are the controls difficult?

No. Just tap the screen to place a bomb, so you can start playing right away with one hand.

Is it free to play?

Yes. It is free to download and play, and it shows ads. A one-time in-app purchase is available to remove ads.

Can I continue after a game over?

You can optionally watch an ad to revive your blocks and continue.

Which devices are supported?

It is coming to iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.0 or later, and to Android 8.0 or later. The game is available in English, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, and Indonesian.
