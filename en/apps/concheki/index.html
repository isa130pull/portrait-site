ConChekiAn app for concafe visits

ConCheki is a stamp card and photo album for visits to concafes in Yonago. Collect cast avatars with the points you earn at the cafe, and keep your cheki (instant) photos together on your phone.

The app is available in Japanese.

Supported cafes

You can use ConCheki at Melty Candy and CheersCruise in Yonago, Tottori, Japan.

What is a concafe?

Concafe is short for “concept cafe.” These cafes and bars have a particular theme, reflected in their costumes and interiors. The two cafes below take their inspiration from maids and cruise ships.

You can chat with maids over food and drinks at Melty Candy. The bar has a blue interior and a stage for singing and dance performances.

Using ConCheki

Visit stamps

Scan the cafe’s QR code to earn stamps and points. When you check in, the app uses your location to confirm that you are at a supported cafe.

Cast avatar gacha

Spend your points on gacha draws to collect avatars of the cafe’s cast members. You can choose a favorite avatar as your profile picture in the app.

Cheki photo book

Scan your cheki prints or add photos from your phone to build a photo album in the app. Organize them by date, cast member, and cafe. The photos are stored on your device.

FAQ

Which cafes can I use it at?

ConCheki supports Melty Candy and CheersCruise in Yonago, Tottori, Japan. The cafe introductions include details about each cafe and links to their official information.

Is the app free?

The app is free, with optional in-app purchases.

Why does the app need my location?

The app uses your location to confirm that you are visiting a supported cafe and to prevent false check-ins. It does not track your movements or use your location for advertising.

Where are my cheki photos stored?

Cheki photos you scan or add from your phone are saved on your device. These photos are not uploaded to an external server.
