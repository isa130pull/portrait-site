FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where can I use ConCheki?

It is currently supported at selected concafes in Yonago, Tottori, Japan. More participating stores are planned.

Is the app free?

The app is free to download and use, with optional in-app purchases.

Why does check-in use location information?

Location is used only to verify a visit when the QR code is scanned and to prevent misuse. It is not used for movement tracking or advertising.

Are manually added cheki photos uploaded?

No. Cheki photos you add manually remain on your device and are not uploaded externally.
