ConChekiA fan app for concafes in Yonago

Earn stamps and points every time you visit, and collect avatars of your favorite cast members. Free to download.

The app is available in Japanese.

Supported cafes in Yonago, Tottori

  • Melty Candy
  • CheersCruise
  • CAPRICE

What you can do

Swipe left or right to see all five features.

  1. Visit stamps

    Scan the cafe’s QR code to earn stamps and points.

  2. Avatar gacha

    Spend points on gacha draws to collect your favorite cast members in different outfits.

  3. 3D diorama & AR

    Display your avatars in a 3D model of the cafe or around you in AR.

  4. Cheki book

    Scan your cheki prints and sort them by date or cast member. Photos stay on your device.

  5. Rankings

    Your visits and collections earn trophies, and you can compare them in national rankings.

FAQ

Which cafes can I use it at?

Melty Candy, CheersCruise, and CAPRICE in Yonago, Tottori, Japan. See Supported cafes for details.

What is a concafe?

Short for “concept cafe.” These cafes and bars have a theme, such as maids or cruise ships, reflected in their costumes and interiors.

Is the app free?

Yes. An optional five-draw gacha costs ¥1,000.

Why does the app need my location?

To confirm that you are at a supported cafe and to prevent false check-ins. It is not used to track your movements or for advertising.

Where are my cheki photos stored?

On your device. Photos you add are not uploaded to an external server.
