What is a concafe?

Short for “concept cafe,” a concafe is a cafe or bar built around a theme, reflected in its costumes and atmosphere. Themes range from maids to cruise ships. ConCheki helps you record visits and memories with your favorite cast members at participating cafes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do visit stamps and the cheki photo book differ?

Checking in at a participating cafe records visits and earns stamps and points. Scan your cheki prints or add photos to organize them by date, cast member, and cafe on your device. You can revisit both visits and photo memories in one app.

What should I check before my first visit?

Check that the cafe participates. Currently, ConCheki supports Melty Candy and CheersCruise in Yonago, Tottori, Japan. At the cafe, open Check In, scan the QR code, and use location information to confirm the store.

Where can I use ConCheki?

You can use it at Melty Candy and CheersCruise in Yonago, Tottori, Japan. Visit the supported cafe introductions for each cafe's concept and official information.

Is the app free?

The app is free to download and use, with optional in-app purchases.

Why does check-in use location information?

Location is used only to verify a visit when the QR code is scanned and to prevent misuse. It is not used for movement tracking or advertising.

Are manually added cheki photos uploaded?

No. Cheki photos you add manually remain on your device and are not uploaded externally.
