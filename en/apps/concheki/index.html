What is a concafe?

A concafe, short for concept cafe, is a cafe or bar with its own theme, costumes, and atmosphere.

Supported cafe

Maid&Bar めるてぃ×きゃんでぃ
37 Asahimachi, Yonago, Tottori, Japan

A maid bar with conversation, live singing and dance performances, and food and drinks in a blue-themed interior.

FAQ

Which cafes are supported?

Melty Candy and CheersCruise in Yonago, Japan. Explore the cafes.

Is the app free?

The app is free, with optional in-app purchases.

Why use location?

Location confirms your visit and prevents misuse. It is not used for movement tracking or advertising.

Where are photos stored?

Manually added photos stay on your device and are not uploaded.
