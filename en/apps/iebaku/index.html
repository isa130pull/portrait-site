Controls and shot upgrades

Tap to fire

Tap the screen to shoot at a spot in the room. Aim at furniture and appliances, and cause as much damage as you can within 30 seconds.

Start a chain reaction

Furniture hit by a shot can fly into other objects and destroy them too. Try aiming at clusters of objects to pull more of the room into the damage.

Upgrade your shots

Spend the money you earn on power, shot size, bounce, and other upgrades. Improve your shots as you work through 11 stages, then try time attacks and score rankings.

Frequently asked questions

How does the 30-second browser challenge differ from the app?

The web version is a simple moving-target tap game. The full app lets you destroy furniture, appliances, and houses across 11 stages, earn money for shot upgrades, and try time attacks and score rankings. Web scores do not carry over to the app.

Should I choose House Breaker or SushiBomb?

Choose House Breaker for furniture, appliances, shot upgrades, and 11 stages. For collecting 36 kinds of sushi and destroying buildings and streets, take a look at SushiBomb. Both use simple tap controls.

How do I play?

Tap the screen to fire a shot. There are no complicated commands, so you can start playing immediately.

How long is one session?

A standard run lasts 30 seconds, making it easy to complete a session during a commute or a short wait.

What can I keep working toward?

The game includes 11 stages, shot upgrades, time attack, and score rankings.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The core game is free to play and is available from the App Store and Google Play.
