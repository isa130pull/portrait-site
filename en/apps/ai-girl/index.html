A FEW MORE THINGS

Good to know

How does the quiz work?

Choose your favorite from four characters in questions 1–5, three in questions 6–7, and two in questions 8–10. The app uses your choices to suggest your type from 149 characters.

What can I collect?

Discover 149 characters, each with a name and voice. Build affinity to unlock additional voices and set a favorite character.

What is the daily destined character?

It is a featured character that changes each day. Open the app to see who it is today.

How is the Web demo different?

The Web demo uses 16 characters and five questions to suggest a style. It is a separate, simplified experience: results are not saved or linked to the app, and it does not unlock characters, voices, or affinity.

Is it free?

The app is free to download and play, with an optional ad-free plan. Check your store for current availability and details. The app screen images on this page are in Japanese.
