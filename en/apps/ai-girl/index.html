Frequently asked questions

How does the five-question web quiz differ from the app?

This page uses five either-or questions to return one of four preference types. The full app uses 10 choices to find a character from a cast of 100, with names, voices, and a gallery to collect. Web results are separate from the app’s results.

How can I discover characters I have not met?

Try different choices in the app and take the quiz again. Each character you discover is added to the gallery, so you can revisit your collection as you work toward finding all 100.

How long does the app’s quiz take?

There are 10 either-or questions. Simply tap the character you prefer, and you can reach the result in a short session.

How many characters are there?

There are 100. Each has a name and voice and is added to the gallery when you discover them.

Can I take the quiz more than once?

Yes. Change your choices and repeat the quiz to find characters you have not met yet.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The core game is free to play and is available from the App Store and Google Play.
