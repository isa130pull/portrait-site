How Christmas bingo works

Instead of numbers, each square holds a holiday prompt such as “Santa Claus” or “Wrapped present.” When a caller reads prompts aloud, it plays just like classic bingo. You can also play spot-it bingo by marking prompts you find in decorations, movies, or picture books.

This tool makes word bingo cards with your own prompts. It does not create 1–75 number bingo cards or draw numbers.

How to run call-out Christmas bingo

  1. Choose a 5×5 card and apply the Christmas template. Edit any prompt you like.
  2. In “Print multiple cards or save a PDF”, choose four cards and select Print / PDF. Each time you print, you get new shuffled cards, so repeat until everyone has one.
  3. The caller writes the 25 prompts on slips of paper, puts them in a hat, and reads them out one at a time.
  4. Players mark each prompt that is called.
  5. The first player to complete a row, column, or diagonal shouts “Bingo!”

Ideas for different groups

  • Classrooms: Use a 3×3 card for younger children, or swap in vocabulary words for an English lesson.
  • Family movie night: Mark prompts as they appear in a Christmas movie and see who gets bingo first.
  • Office holiday party: Add prompts about your team’s year to start conversations between colleagues.
  • Far-away family: Send a share link and play on the same card over a video call.

How to print Christmas bingo cards or save a PDF

  1. Apply the template, then edit the title and prompts.
  2. Choose a card font and check the result in Preview. “Bold impact” is easy for children to read.
  3. In “Print multiple cards or save a PDF”, choose two or four cards and select Print / PDF. Each card uses a different order.
  4. Choose “Save as PDF” in your print dialog to keep a copy for sharing or printing later.

In call-out bingo, identical cards win at the same time. Print again to create more shuffled cards so that every player has a different layout.

Frequently asked questions

Can young children play?
Yes. Choose a 3×3 card and shorten the prompts. Check Preview to make sure the text is large enough before printing.
Can I make number bingo cards?
No. This tool is for word and prompt bingo. It does not generate 1–75 number cards or draw numbers.
Is it free? Do I need an account?
It is free and needs no account. You can create, print, save as PDF or image, and share cards at no cost.
Where are my prompts saved?
Your card is saved in this browser. A share link contains the card data, so do not include private information.
