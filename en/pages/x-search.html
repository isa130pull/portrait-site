What is the X Advanced Search Builder?

This free tool turns your form selections into an advanced X (formerly Twitter) search query. Combine useful search operators without memorizing their syntax.

Supported search operators

  • from: find posts from a specific account
  • to: find replies sent to a specific account
  • since: / until: narrow results to a date range
  • filter:images show posts with images
  • min_faves: set a minimum number of likes
  • -filter:replies exclude replies

Features

  • Free to use with no sign-up
  • Quick presets for common searches
  • Advanced mode for combining multiple filters
  • Copy the completed query with one click
