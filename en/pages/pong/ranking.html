About POPONG

POPONG is a free classic Pong game for your browser. Race to seven points against the CPU in Normal or Hard mode. Keyboard and touch controls make it playable on desktop and mobile.

Submit your clear time to the online leaderboard and compare it with players around the world. No registration is required.
