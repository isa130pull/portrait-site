What is setlist bingo?

Setlist bingo turns concert predictions into a game. Add songs you expect to hear, then mark them as the show unfolds. You can also use spaces for stage effects, costumes, talk segments, guest appearances, or anything else you want to predict. The same card maker works for streams, parties, classroom activities, and other events.

How to play

  1. Choose a 3×3 or 5×5 card and enter songs or prediction prompts.
  2. Optionally enable the free center space, then shuffle the entries.
  3. Use Preview to check the finished card with its title and selected font.
  4. Use “Copy share link” for friends or “Save image” for social media.
  5. Switch to Play mode during the event and mark matching spaces. Completed bingo lines are counted automatically.

Choosing 3×3 or 5×5

  • 3×3: Best for shorter concerts and quick setlist prediction games.
  • 5×5: Better for longer shows, standard bingo cards, and event activities.
  • Free center: The center space counts as marked as soon as it is enabled.

Saving and sharing

Your entries are saved in this browser. A share link contains the card data, so do not enter personal or private information. Recipients can open the same shared card in either the English or Japanese version.
