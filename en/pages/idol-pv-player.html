What is the Japanese Underground Idol MV Player?

This free browser app keeps Japanese underground idol music videos playing in a fair, endlessly refillable queue. It runs entirely in your browser with your own YouTube Data API key.

Two collection modes

  • Festival performers (default) — Resolve the built-in list of 199 performers to official channels once, then fetch recent MVs efficiently from cached channel matches.
  • Keyword search — Discover new artists while filtering common noise such as game fan works, VTubers, and cover videos.

Features

  • Free to use with no sign-up; bring your own API key
  • Fair queue generation that limits consecutive videos from one group
  • Continuous playback in a temporary YouTube playlist
  • Theater mode for a larger player
